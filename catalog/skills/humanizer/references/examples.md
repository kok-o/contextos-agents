# Editing examples

Use these as judgment examples rather than replacement rules.

## Redundant promotion

Source: "Our revolutionary dashboard empowers teams to unlock productivity.
It is not just a dashboard, it is the future of collaboration. Export weekly
task counts as CSV."

Revision: "Export weekly task counts as CSV."

The first two sentences add no supported capability. Do not add a time-saving
percentage, customer quote or personal anecdote to replace them.

## Russian precision

Source: "Данная функция осуществляет обеспечение бесшовного экспорта отчётов.
Экспорт доступен только администратору."

Revision: "Функция экспортирует отчёты. Экспорт доступен только администратору."

Keep the access restriction. Do not replace "администратор" with "участник".
Keep a sentence such as "Отчёт — это снимок данных на момент выгрузки": the dash
has a grammatical role, and the time qualification conveys information.

## Preserve uncertainty and voice

Source: "In this one trial, median latency fell from 120 ms to 108 ms. We have
not tested peak load. I would try a longer run before changing the defaults."

Keep these claims and the personal judgment. Turning this into "10% faster
under all workloads" adds an unsupported generalization and drops a limitation.
